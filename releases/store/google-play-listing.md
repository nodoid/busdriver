## Full description (4000)

**The 1985 timetable, back on the road.**

Bus Driver puts you in the cab of a Scarthorpe bus on a working shift. Read the
blind, keep to the timetable, watch the traffic and get your passengers where
they are going without putting the bus in a wall.

SIXTEEN ROUTES, FOUR HERITAGE RUNS
From the High Street Hopper to the Night Bus N1, every route runs from a
terminus, calls at the town's stops and turns round for the return trip. Four
heritage services are worked exactly as they were in 1985.

THREE BUSES, THREE JOBS
Take the nippy minibus with sixteen seats, the standard thirty-two seat
single-decker, or the fifty-six seat double-decker that earns the most fares but
turns like a cathedral.

THREE WAYS TO WORK A SHIFT
Easy announces every junction and keeps the timetable kind. Medium only calls
the turns you actually need and adds real traffic. Hard gives you a tight
schedule, bad weather and a small bus.

A TOWN THAT KEEPS MOVING
Cars, taxis, vans and lorries work the left-hand lanes. Terminus junctions are
signal controlled: while a bus is in the yard, nothing else comes in. Rain, fog
and snow change the grip, the stopping distance and the view.

TIMETABLES AND FARES
Every stop has a due time. Arrive inside tolerance for punctuality points, drop
off and pick up in the same call, and keep an eye on the free seats. Damage is
deducted from the score at the end of the shift.

MADE FOR A PHONE
One hand, landscape, no text entry and no adverts. GO latches the throttle so
you can steer with your thumb; BRAKE holds the bus; REV backs you out of
trouble.

• 16 routes over a working town map
• 3 bus types with their own seats, length and handling
• 3 difficulties, each with its own traffic, weather and guidance
• Offline. No accounts, no adverts, no in-app purchases, no data collection.
