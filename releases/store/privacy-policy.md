# Bus Driver — privacy policy

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

Bus Driver is a single-player driving game published by Scarthorpe Games.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Bus Driver does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- No account, sign-in or profile.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or notification access.
- No network requests at all: the game runs entirely offline.

## What is stored on the device

Two things, both on the device and both removed when the app is deleted:

- the app's own preferences (last bus chosen, last difficulty chosen);
- a short diagnostic log the app writes if it starts with a problem, used only
  to work out what went wrong. It never leaves the device and is not uploaded
  anywhere.

## Children

The app has no data collection of any kind, so it collects nothing from
children. The content is a bus driving simulation rated 4+ / Everyone.

## Third parties

There are no third-party services, SDKs or trackers in the app.
